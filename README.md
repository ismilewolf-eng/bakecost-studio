## 💡 Why BakeCost Studio?

Most home bakers and cottage bakeries underprice their goods by charging only for raw ingredients while giving away active decorating labor, oven electricity, and packaging materials for free.

**BakeCost Studio** solves bakery batch economics directly inside your browser with zero server tracking.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy (Local Storage)**
   - All proprietary formulas, secret recipes, raw ingredient prices, and profit margins stay strictly on your device. Zero cloud sync, zero telemetry.
2. **⚖️ 40+ Built-in Baking Ingredient Density Converters**
   - Automatically convert between volumetric measures (Cups, Tablespoons, Teaspoons, Milliliters) and weight (Grams, Ounces, Pounds) using empirical bulk densities.
3. **🔥 Baking Moisture Loss & Trim Waste Accounting**
   - Accurate cost calculation factoring prep trim yield % (e.g. berry stems, citrus zest) and oven moisture evaporation loss (typically 5% to 18%).
4. **📦 Comprehensive 4-Part Cost Allocation**
   - Raw Ingredients + Primary/Secondary Packaging + Active Hands-on Labor + Kitchen Overhead/Utilities.
5. **📈 Real-Time Margin & Target Food Cost Slider**
   - Set target food cost percentages (e.g., the commercial 28% sweet spot) to instantly see suggested retail prices per serving and per batch.
6. **🖨️ Printable Recipe Production Cards**
   - One-click high-resolution PNG image download and printable cards for kitchen binders.
7. **📱 Offline PWA Support**
   - Add to iOS or Android home screen and calculate costs anywhere without an internet connection.
