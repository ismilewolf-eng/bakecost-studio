# BakeCost Studio (Bakery & Recipe Cost Calculator)

A professional, privacy-first, client-side recipe costing and food pricing calculator designed for international independent bakers, home cottage bakeries, and small café owners.

## 🌟 Key Features
1. **100% Client-Side Privacy (Local Storage)**: All proprietary recipes and cost numbers remain strictly on your local device.
2. **40+ Built-in Baking Ingredient Densities**: Instant auto-conversion between Weight (g/kg/oz/lb) and Volume (Cup/Tbsp/Tsp/ml) based on standard culinary density tables.
3. **Baking Loss & Yield / Trim Rates**: Accurate accounting for peeling/trimming prep loss and oven moisture evaporation.
4. **Three-Tier Cost Allocation**: Ingredients + Packaging Supplies + Labor Time + Kitchen Electricity/Overhead.
5. **Two-Way Real-time Pricing Engine**: Slide the Target Food Cost % (15% ~ 50%) to compute suggested price, or type a custom retail price to evaluate net profit and gross margin %.
6. **Printable Recipe Cards & PNG Export**: One-click high-resolution PNG image download or PDF print sheet.
7. **Offline PWA Support**: Installable on iOS/Android home screens and fully functional offline.
8. **Data Backup & Restore**: Export and import full recipe data in JSON format.
