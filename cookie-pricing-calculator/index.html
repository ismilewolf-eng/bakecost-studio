Custom Cookie Economics

How to Price Custom Decorated Cookies Per Dozen

Decorated royal icing cookies are one of the most labor-intensive confectionery items in the baking industry. Mixing multiple icing consistencies, custom dye coloring, drying time, and intricate piping mean labor accounts for up to 80% of your production cost.

Cookie Costing & Per-Dozen Pricing Formula

Dozen Price = (Butter Dough + Royal Icing + Bags/Ribbon) + (Piping Hours × Hourly Rate) + 25% Profit Margin

Step-by-Step Production Costing Methodology

1. Standardize Cookie Thickness & Cutters

Rolling dough to a consistent 5/16" or 3/8" with guide rings ensures predictable yield per batch (typically 18 - 24 standard 3.5" cookies per dough batch).

2. Cost Meringue Powder & Food Gel Dyes

Royal icing made with premium meringue powder costs roughly $4.00 to $6.00 per batch. High-pigment gel pastes and edible luster dusts add up quickly.

3. Tier Pricing by Design Complexity

Never charge a flat rate per dozen. Create at least 3 tiers: Basic (1-2 colors, flood only), Detailed (3-5 colors, lettering, simple florals), and Elaborate (hand-painting, gold accents, metallics).

4. Individual Packaging & Heat-Sealing

Individual food-safe cellophane sleeves, heat-sealing, bow ribbons, and custom ingredient compliance stickers add $0.35 to $0.65 per cookie ($4.20 - $7.80 per dozen).

5. Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)

Enforce a minimum order of 1 dozen (or 2 dozen for complex themes) to justify mixing 4 to 6 separate icing piping bags and colors.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average price for a dozen decorated sugar cookies?

Nationwide in the US, custom decorated cookies range from $48 to $72 per dozen for standard themes, and $80 to $120+ per dozen for highly detailed corporate logos or wedding favors.

Why do cookies take so much labor?

Unlike cakes, each cookie is an individual canvas. Creating stiff, outline, and flood consistencies, tinting custom colors, and dehydrating between layers requires extensive hands-on focus.

How do I price individually packaged party favors?

Add $0.50 per cookie for heat-sealed cellophane packaging with custom logo stickers, and $1.00 per cookie if tied with silk or organza ribbon.
